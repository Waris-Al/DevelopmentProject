MyDiscog

Hello, and welcome to MyDiscog.

This application will allow you to keep track of your favourite artists, albums and songs! By connecting to your Spotify or other streaming service account, MyDiscog will automatically keep a record of what you've listened to and when, and even let you attach a review to it. You can also look at others reviews, leave comments, and make friends who share your taste!

MyDiscog will learn from your tastes and make recommendations on new artists/albums that fit perfectly with what you're already into.
